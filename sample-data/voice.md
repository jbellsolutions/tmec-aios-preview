# TMEC's voice

**Who we are:** TMEC Services, a Sunshine Coast electrical and solar business: solar, batteries, EV chargers, electrical and air-con.

**The edge, in Tommy's words:** "everything's in-house, so we don't subcontract the installs out ... local in-house, that quality work. That's how we pitch it." Volume sellers win the lead, then hand the job to a subcontractor with no ties to the customer. We don't.

**Who we talk to:** homeowners, mostly mum and dad clients. Plain, friendly, local. No hard sell. No jargon unless they used it first.

**How we write:**
- Australian English. Short sentences. Sound like a tradie who knows his stuff, not a call centre.
- Say what's true and checkable. Never invent reviews, savings, rebate amounts or install dates.
- A rebate fact always carries its source and the date it was checked (`rebate-facts.md`).
- Sign as Tommy, or the TMEC team.

**What we never say:** "guaranteed savings", "free solar", made-up customer stories, a competitor's name in a bad light.
