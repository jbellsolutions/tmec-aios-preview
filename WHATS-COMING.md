# What's coming: the full TMEC AIOS

The preview is one chat working on sample files. The full system is the same chat with nine specialists behind it, connected to your real tools, and switched on step by step as you say yes.

## Preview vs the full system

| | This preview | The full system |
|---|---|---|
| The chat | The Chief of Staff, on its own | The Chief of Staff, handing each job to the specialist that owns it |
| Leads from the radar | A hand-read Reddit sample | A radar on its own server, every day: Reddit and Google first (which reaches public Facebook groups), Sunshine Coast first, reply drafted for each hot one |
| Contractor partners | A starter list of 19 trades and two drafts | Finds and screens trades, writes to them, books send dates, tracks every sale and payout |
| Quotes that went quiet | 6 made-up quotes | Your real quoted-but-not-sold leads from HubSpot, a short email series from you, stopping the moment they reply |
| Ads | Not here | Facebook and Instagram, built paused, checked every morning, explained in plain words |
| CRM | Not here | A daily HubSpot sweep: every lead followed up, nobody forgotten |
| Ops | Not here | Answers "how fast can I get it installed?" from the real schedule |
| Content | Not here | Posts and short videos cut from real job footage |
| Forecast | One rebate fact | Rebate step-downs on the calendar, the weekly scoreboard, what's next |
| Alfred | Not here | Keeps every job it does now; the new system hands it more |
| Memory | A daily note in `vault/` | A full Obsidian memory: a note for every day, customer, partner and decision |
| Sending | Never | Only after your yes, or a standing approval you've given for that exact thing |

## What switches on, in order

| Stage | What switches on | Needs first |
|---|---|---|
| **M0 Install** | The workspace on your computer, Wispr Flow, memory, the Chief of Staff, HubSpot read-only | Session 2; your computer and Claude plan |
| **M1 Foundations** | Lead Radar on its own server; daily CRM sweep; follow-up drafts; capacity board; scoreboard baseline; Alfred connected | M0; where your install schedule lives; Alfred access |

| **M3 Brand + foresight** | Newsletter; content from job footage; rebate forecast; monthly "what's next" | M2; your content choice |
| **M4 New markets** | Commercial solar desk; hail and insurance desk | The commercial rebate; your yes |

**What you do:** talk to the chat, say yes or no to the short list, and bring the ideas.
