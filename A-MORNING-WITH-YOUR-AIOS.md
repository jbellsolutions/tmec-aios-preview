# A morning with your AIOS

What a normal morning looks like once your system is running. You talk (by voice, with Wispr Flow), the Chief of Staff answers, and the specialists behind it have already done the work overnight.

**About this example:** the radar posts are real, from a hand-read sample of public Reddit posts (28 October 2026, no usernames kept), and the partner list is real, from OpenStreetMap. The quiet quotes are made up for practice. Ads, the newsletter and content aren't switched on in the preview. Nothing here was sent to anyone.

---

**You:** Morning. What's going on?

**Chief of Staff:**

> **Needs you (3)**
> 1. Radar found 4 people in Queensland ready to buy solar and batteries. Replies drafted. Want to read them?
> 2. Partners has a first hello ready for 5 Sunshine Coast trades. Okay to line them up for your review?
> 3. 6 quotes went quiet. A follow-up is drafted for each, matched to why they stalled. Read them?
>
> **Since yesterday**
> - Radar: 23 real leads in the sample, 4 hot, all 4 in Queensland (north Brisbane, Brisbane, Gold Coast).
> - Partners: 19 trades on the starter list: builders, kitchens, plumbers, pool shops, pest control and more. No electricians.
> - Quiet quotes: 6, sorted by why they stalled: price 1, timing 2, battery unsure 1, comparing 1, went quiet 1.
> - Ads, newsletter, content: built, switched off.
>
> **Coming up:** the battery rebate steps down from 6.8 to 5.7 certificates per kWh for installs from 1 January 2027 (Clean Energy Regulator, checked 28 Oct 2026). Worth a campaign before then?

**You:** Yes to 1. Show me the best one.

**Chief of Staff:**

> A homeowner who just bought a place in Brisbane north. Old solar hot water panels on the roof, wants new solar and a battery, and is asking whether an electrician can take the old panels off or a plumber has to come first.
>
> *Drafted reply:*
>
> Congrats on the new place. Short answer: it's usually both trades. The water side of an old solar hot water system is plumbing work, so a licensed plumber disconnects and caps it, and an electrician looks after any electric booster plus the new solar and battery. Worth asking on every quote: does it include taking the old panels off, sealing up the old roof holes, and what replaces your hot water? A good installer organises the plumber so you're not juggling trades. Full disclosure, I'm with TMEC Services on the Sunshine Coast. We do our own installs, no subcontractors. Happy to answer anything here.
>
> Post it as is, change it, or skip?

**You:** Make it a bit shorter and post it.

**Chief of Staff:**

> Shortened to 70 words and ready to copy. You post replies yourself from TMEC's account: the system never posts as you. Say "posted" when it's up, and Radar watches for their answer, tells you, and passes them to your CRM as a new lead.

**You:** Yes to 2 as well. Show me one.

**Chief of Staff:**

> Creative Kitchens in Nambour. Their customers are homeowners putting money into their homes.
>

>
> Subject: solar for your customers
>
> Hi there,
>
> I came across Creative Kitchens while looking at kitchen renovators around Nambour.
>
> I'm Tommy from TMEC Services on the Sunshine Coast. We install solar, batteries and EV chargers with our own installers, not subcontractors.

>
> Open to a quick call to see if it suits you?
>
> *I've also written the email they'd send their own customers. Nothing goes out until the Australian spam rules are checked and you've set the partner fee. That's on your list for Tuesday.*

**You:** Good. Oh, and can it chase the hail insurance jobs too?

**Chief of Staff:**

> The hail and insurance desk is planned for a later milestone (M4). I've added "chase the hail insurance jobs" to your big list in your words, so it comes up on Tuesday.

**You:** Sweet. That's it.

**Chief of Staff:**

> Done. Today's note is in your memory (`vault/daily/`): what came in, what you said yes to, and the new idea. Your evening brief comes at the end of the day.

## What you just saw

- **One chat.** You didn't open HubSpot, Reddit, Facebook or a spreadsheet. You talked.
- **The work was already done.** Overnight, the specialists found the leads, wrote the drafts and sorted the follow-ups.
- **You only steer.** Every decision came to you as a yes or no.
- **Nothing goes out without your yes.** Posts, emails and ad spend all wait for you.
- **Nothing gets forgotten.** Every idea goes on one list, and every day is in a memory you can open in Obsidian.
