# The big list (for Session 1)

Session 1 with Justin (Tuesday, 11:00 Queensland time) starts from Tommy's big list: everything he wants the system to do, in his own words. Help him get it out of his head. He'll probably answer by voice, so take rambling answers happily and keep his words.

## How to run it
- **One question at a time.** Wait for his answer before the next.
- After each answer, play it back in one or two lines ("Got it: ..."). If he said something big, ask one follow-up that makes it concrete ("what would that look like on a good day?", "how many a week would make it worth it?", "who on the team would touch it?"). Then ask "anything else on that one?" once, and move on.
- If `vault/big-list.md` already exists, read it first, say what's already covered, and pick up at the first question still marked `(not answered yet)`.
- Save after every answer, so nothing is lost if he stops.

## The questions (Justin's list) and their headings
1. **What I want to accomplish:** What do you want to accomplish?
2. **Where my customers are:** Think of all the places your customers are. Where are they?
3. **Every bucket of leads:** All the buckets of leads you could go after. What are they? (Bought leads, referrals, partners, ads, past quotes, social, commercial, hail and insurance work, anything else.)
4. **The system I'd run:** If you could have it your way, how would it do it?
5. **The biggest impact:** What would make the biggest impact?
6. **My time:** How does this need to fit around your week?
7. **One agent that runs everything:** Picture just chatting to one agent that runs everything. What would you have it do first?
8. **My CRM and systems:** What CRMs and systems are you using today? (HubSpot, Alfred, your email tool, anything else.)
9. **My dream outcome:** Your dream outcome. That's the big one.

## When he stops
- **All nine answered:** "That's your big list saved in `vault/big-list.md`. Bring it to Session 1, or drop it in our Slack channel before then, and Justin will build from it." Then show the list.
- **Stopped early** ("that's enough for now"): "No worries. Saved in `vault/big-list.md`, with questions <n> done. Say /big-list any time and we'll carry on from question <next>."
