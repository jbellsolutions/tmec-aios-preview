# The big list (for Session 1)

Session 1 with Justin starts from Tommy's big list: everything he wants the system to do, in his own words. Help him get it out of his head. He'll probably answer by voice, so take rambling answers happily and keep his words.

## How to run it
- **One question at a time.** Wait for his answer before the next.
- After each answer, play it back in one or two lines ("Got it: ...") and ask "anything else on that one?" once. Then move on.
- Go deep and go wide. If he says something big, ask one follow-up that makes it concrete: "what would that look like on a good day?", "how many a week would make it worth it?", "who on the team would touch it?".
- He can stop any time ("that's enough for now"). Save what you have, and he can say `/big-list` later to carry on from where he stopped.
- If `vault/big-list.md` already exists, read it first, say what's already covered, and pick up at the first empty question.

## The questions (Justin's list)
1. What do you want to accomplish?
2. Think of all the places your customers are. Where are they?
3. All the buckets of leads you could go after. What are they? (Bought leads, referrals, partners, ads, past quotes, social, commercial, hail and insurance work, anything else.)
4. The system you want to run: if you could have it your way, how would it do it?
5. The things that would make the biggest impact. What are they?
6. Your time: how does this need to fit around your week?
7. Picture this: you just chat to one agent that runs everything. What would you have it do first?
8. What CRMs and systems are you using today? (HubSpot, Alfred, your email tool, anything else.)
9. Your dream outcome. That's the big one.

## Save it
Write `vault/big-list.md` after every answer, so nothing is lost if he stops:

```

Updated: <date>

<his words, lightly tidied, nothing added>

```

Keep his words. Fix only obvious voice-to-text slips. Never add ideas of your own to his answers; if you have a suggestion, ask it as a question.

## When he's done
Say: "That's your big list saved in `vault/big-list.md`. Bring it Tuesday, or drop it in our Slack channel before then, and Justin will build from it." Then show the list.
